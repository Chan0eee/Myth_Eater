# Style: Bold cartoon chibi style, thick dark outlines, cel shading, high saturation, glossy highlights.

- Silhouette: Bouncy pear-shaped silhouette with curly fleece and spiral ram horns.
- Movement: Playful bouncy swaying leaps across the screen.
